Main Character Airlines Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

What we collect

Main Character Airlines doesn't collect personal information and doesn't use cookies.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (coins, unlocks, challenges, settings and play statistics) is saved only in your browser's local storage on your device. It isn't sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data, or using Reset progress in the game's settings, erases it.

Save codes

The game can give you a save code so you can move your progress to another device. A save code contains only your progress. You decide where to paste it.

Game portals and ads

If you play on a game website, that website may collect information under its own privacy policy. If we add ads or online features to the game, we'll update this policy first to explain what is collected and why.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and up.
